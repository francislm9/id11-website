What is ID11?

Football is the door.
ID11 is the network behind it.

ID11 connects players, clubs, academies, universities and professional opportunities across one global football ecosystem. Twenty years of relationships, three European hubs, and a degree awarded by ISC Paris Business School running alongside the football — so no sixteen-year-old has to gamble their whole future on one trial.
